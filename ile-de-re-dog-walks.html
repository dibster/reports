Vauban Ramparts & Port Loop

Zero Driving
🚶 Direct from Villa Vauban (Venelle de Clerjotte)

Walk directly out onto the star-shaped UNESCO ramparts and through Parc de la Barbette. Follow the wide, flat grassy outer moats and glacis overlooking the Pertuis Breton ocean out to the Citadel, then loop around the historic harbour.

Brody & Ivy Fit: Soft grassy moats are gentle on paws and flat for Ivy. Plenty of space and fresh ocean breezes for Brody on a lead. Finishes at dog-friendly harbour café terraces.
  • 📏 Distance / Time: ~3–5 km / 45–60 min loop
  • 📜 Rules: Dogs allowed on lead across all ramparts, glacis, port, and parks.
  • ⚠️ Note: Plage de la Cible (town beach) bans dogs, but the coastal path running above it is open.

Plage des Gouillauds

Open All Day
🚗 ~10 min drive south (Le Bois-Plage-en-Ré)

The primary authorized dog beach on the south coast, located between Les Grenettes and Gros Jonc. Access via Pas d'Antioche (near Camping Antioche) or Chemin des Gouillauds.

Brody & Ivy Fit: Unrestricted wide Atlantic sands where Brody can properly stretch his legs. Great firm sand at low tide for easy Dachshund trotting.
  • 📜 September Rules: Explicitly permitted on lead all year, including during summer and September (unlike Gros Jonc which bans dogs until 15 Sept).
  • 🌊 Terrain: Open ocean beach, sand dunes, and rolling Atlantic surf.

Ars-en-Ré Wild Beaches

Restrictions Lifted 1 Sept
🚗 ~15–20 min drive west

Summer restrictions lifted on 1 September across the municipality's wilder beaches: Plage du Martray, Plage de la Marielle, Pointe de Grignon, and Radia.

Brody & Ivy Fit: Quiet and peaceful with low visitor counts in September. Watch paws around rocky low-tide patches.
  • 📜 September Rules: Legally authorized on lead 1 September through 30 June. (Plage de la Grange reopens 1 Oct).
  • 🌊 Terrain: Natural wild beaches, rocky intertidal foreshore (estran), and salt marsh edges.

Plage de la Conche des Baleines

19:00 – 09:00 Allowed
🚗 ~25 min drive northwest (Saint-Clément-des-Baleines)

The island's most dramatic curved Atlantic beach, stretching for kilometres backed by high dunes. During the summer period (until 15 September), dogs are welcomed during evening and early morning hours.

Brody & Ivy Fit: Unbeatable for an evening sunset run or crisp early morning walk when the entire bay is virtually deserted.
  • ⏰ Permitted Hours: 19:00 to 09:00 daily (until 15 Sept). Banned 09:00–19:00.
  • 💡 Tip: Park near Phare des Baleines or Pas de Zanuck for direct dune path access.

Bois de Trousse-Chemise & Lizay

Full Canopy Shade
🚗 ~25–30 min drive north (Les Portes-en-Ré)

Shaded maritime pine and holm oak woods on the northern tip. Soft needle-covered sandy paths lead through the forest to the coast overlooking the famous Banc du Bûcheron sandbank.

Brody & Ivy Fit: The best warm-weather walk on the island. Complete sun protection, zero hot tarmac, and soft ground that is very easy on Ivy's legs.
  • 🌲 Terrain: Shaded sand/needle tracks, gentle dunes, coastal pine forest.
  • 🌊 Banc du Bûcheron: At low tide, a 4 km sandbank emerges opposite the beach. Watch incoming tides as currents are strong.
  • ⛔ CRITICAL WARNING: Strictly avoid the adjoining Réserve Naturelle de Lilleau des Niges (dogs 100% prohibited year-round).

Presqu'île de Loix Salt Dikes

Dead Flat & Scenic
🚗 ~15 min drive northwest (Loix)

Walk along the raised earth and gravel dikes (digues) separating active salt pans (marais salants), oyster basins, and the ocean. Peaceful birdlife with herons, egrets, and avocets.

Brody & Ivy Fit: Dead flat walking with no climbs. Walking the pedestrian dikes keeps you clear of the busy paved cycle routes.
  • 📏 Distance: Flexible loops from 2 km to 7 km.
  • 📜 Rules: Must remain on lead at all times to protect nesting and feeding birds.
  • ☀️ Caution: Zero shade across the salt pans—walk early morning or late afternoon to avoid direct heat.

⚠️ Essential Paw & Island Safety

Oyster Shells & Foreshore Rocks

The rocky seabed (estran) exposed at low tide is covered in razor-sharp wild oysters and barnacles. Keep Brody on sandy paths to avoid slicing paw pads.

Zero Fresh Water on Trails

No streams exist on the island. Between salt spray, sea breezes, and sand, both dogs dehydrate fast. Carry a bottle and travel bowl on every walk.

High-Speed Cycleways

Over 110 km of cycleways crisscross the island. Silent electric bikes move quickly around blind bends. Keep leashes short when crossing or walking shared paths.

Lilleau des Niges Nature Reserve

Strictly no dogs permitted year-round (even carried or in bags) within the national bird sanctuary at Les Portes-en-Ré. Fines are strictly enforced.
