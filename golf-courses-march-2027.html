The handicap check changes the shortlist

We’ll use iGolf to get each player’s WHS Handicap Index. It suits golfers who play at different clubs without relying on one home club; the current index and digital record are accessed in the MyEG app. If a player has held an England Golf index before, use the existing membership number so their record carries across. “Around 28 on a good day” is not enough to establish eligibility. First: collect the handicaps

Open the MyEG app and note each player’s current iGolf WHS Handicap Index. Save or open the digital handicap record so it is ready to show the clubs. Then compare the exact index with each club’s rule. A score of 28 on a good day does not tell us the official index.
