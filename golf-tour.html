⛳ Course Breakdown & 28 HCP Suitability

North Course (Best for 28 HCP)Par 71 • 5,752 m

Shortest layout with wide fairways and flat landing zones. Very playable and forgiving for high handicappers.

South Course (Championship)Par 73 • 6,499 m

5-time Spanish Open host. Wide palm-lined corridors with dramatic water hazards on the 18th.

West CoursePar 72 • 5,971 m

Tight mountain layout with severe elevation changes and pine trees. Recommend playing North twice instead for high handicaps.

🥘 Authentic Local Dining & Apres Golf

Cabo de Palos Harbour (8 mins away) Traditional fishing village famous for Caldero Murciano (rich slow-cooked rockfish and rice stew served with homemade alioli) and fresh catch of the day.
Los Belones Village & On-Site • Los Belones (5 mins): Bustling Spanish village with authentic tapas bars, Rioja, and local taverns.
• On-Site: Don Luigi Trattoria (Italian) and Amapola (fine Murcian cuisine).
