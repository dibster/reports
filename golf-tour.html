1. La Cala Resort

Mijas, Costa del Sol, Andalusia, Spain
£520 – £620 per person (3N + 3 rounds)
✈️ Malaga (AGP) (30 mins drive)
☀️ March Weather: 19.5°C high / 10.5°C low (7.5 hrs sun)
⛳ Golf: 54 Championship Holes on-site
🏨 Hotel: 4* Superior + Luxury Thermal Spa

⛳ Championship Courses On-Site

Campo America Par 72 • 6,009 m

Hilly layout with spectacular elevated tee shots overlooking the Sierra de Mijas and the Mediterranean. Wide fairways with dramatic elevation changes.

Campo Asia Par 72 • 5,925 m

The oldest and most strategic course. Demands precision tee shots through narrow valley approaches, elevated greens, and testing water hazards on the 3rd and 5th.

Campo Europa Par 71 • 6,014 m

A flatter course crossing the River Ojen via bridges. Generous fairways, larger greens, and strategic bunkers make it ideal for a relaxed team game.

🚗 Logistics & Access

• Direct Luton Flights: easyJet & Ryanair fly LTN ⇄ AGP daily (approx 2h 45m flight time).
• Airport Transfer: 35 km (30 mins) via AP-7 toll or A-7 coastal highway. Private minibus transfers are widely available (~€35pp return for a group of 8).

🥘 Food & Authenticity

On-Site Andalusian Gastronomy La Terraza serves authentic Iberian cured ham (Jamón Ibérico de Bellota), grilled sea bass, and Andalusian suckling pig. The Clubhouse serves fresh tapas, albóndigas, and Spanish tortilla.
Nearby Authentic Spanish Dining (10–15 mins) • Mijas Pueblo (15 mins): Iconic whitewashed village perched on the cliff; authentic bodegas with tapas, local Pedro Ximenez wines, and views over the coast.
• La Cala de Mijas (10 mins): Seaside village famous for rustic beachfront chiringuitos serving freshly grilled espetos de sardinas (sardines on olive-wood skewers) and seafood paella.

2. Palmares Ocean Living & Golf

Lagos, Western Algarve, Portugal
£560 – £690 per person (3N + 3 rounds)
✈️ Faro (FAO) (50 mins drive)
☀️ March Weather: 19.0°C high / 10.0°C low (7.5 hrs sun)
⛳ Golf: 27 Holes (Robert Trent Jones Jr.)
🏨 Hotel: 5* Boutique Beach Resort & Clubhouse

⛳ 27-Hole Championship Layout

Alvor Loop (9 holes) Par 36 • 3,149 m

Plays through undulating parkland down into a scenic pine valley with dramatic vistas of the Alvor Estuary.

Lagos Loop (9 holes) Par 36 • 3,178 m

Incorporates two lakes and sweeping views of the Bay of Lagos. Strategic doglegs and rolling greens.

Dunes Loop (9 holes) Par 36 • 3,184 m

True coastal links golf playing right along the sands and dunes of Meia Praia beach. Fast, breezy, and exhilarating.

🚗 Logistics & Access

• Direct Luton Flights: easyJet & Ryanair fly LTN ⇄ FAO daily (approx 2h 50m).
• Airport Transfer: 85 km (50 mins) straight along the A22 motorway.
• Town Proximity: Just 5 minutes drive into Lagos historic center.

🥘 Food & Authenticity

Michelin-Star On-Site + Clubhouse Grill Home to AL SUD (1 Michelin Star) offering contemporary Atlantic fine dining. The Clubhouse terrace serves traditional Bacalhau à Brás, garlic prawns, and cold Sagres beer overlooking the bay.
Lagos Town Center (5 mins away) • Historic Lagos: 16th-century walled maritime town filled with lively Portuguese *tascas* and seafood grills.
• Signature Dishes: Cataplana de Marisco (copper-pot seafood stew with clams, prawns, and chorizo), grilled Atlantic sea bream, and Algarvian almond desserts with Medronho.

3. Quinta do Lago & Vale do Lobo

Golden Triangle, Almancil, Algarve, Portugal
£680 – £850 per person (3N + 3 rounds)
✈️ Faro (FAO) (20 mins drive)
☀️ March Weather: 19.2°C high / 10.2°C low (7.5 hrs sun)
⛳ Golf: 5 World-Class Championship Courses
🏨 Hotel: 5* Luxury Resort & Magnolia Hotel

⛳ Championship Courses

Quinta do Lago South Par 72 • 6,488 m

8-time Portuguese Open venue. Carved through umbrella pines with dramatic water hazards on the 15th, 16th, and 17th holes.

Quinta do Lago North Par 72 • 6,126 m

Redesigned by Paul McGinley & Beau Welling. Precision greens, undulating fairways, and strategic bunkering.

Laranjal Course Par 72 • 6,480 m

Set in a former orange grove valley with five independent lakes and immaculate greens conditioning.

🚗 Logistics & Access

• Fastest Transfer: Only 18 km (20 mins) from Faro Airport.
• Accommodation Options: The Magnolia Hotel (retro-chic, lively sports bar) or luxury self-catering golf villas.

🥘 Food & Authenticity

Premium Estate Dining • Casa Velha: Historic fine-dining estate house founded in 1972 serving heritage Portuguese recipes.
• Casa do Lago: Lakeside restaurant specializing in fresh local oysters, turbot, and Portuguese vinho verde.
Local Towns (Almancil & Quarteira — 8 mins) • Authentic Piri-Piri: The surrounding Almancil & Guia area is the birthplace of charcoal-grilled Piri-Piri chicken.
• Quarteira Fish Market: Authentic working harbour taverns serving grilled sea bass, sardines, and local Algarve wines at genuine local prices.

4. La Manga Club

Murcia / Costa Cálida, Spain
£490 – £610 per person (3N + 3 rounds)
✈️ Alicante (ALC) (60 mins) or RMU (30 mins)
☀️ March Weather: 20.2°C high / 9.8°C low (Only 2 rain days)
⛳ Golf: 3 Championship 18-Hole Courses (54 Holes)
🏨 Hotel: Grand Hyatt 5* & Las Lomas Apartments

⛳ 3 Championship 18-Hole Courses

South Course Par 73 • 6,499 m

5-time Spanish Open host. Long, challenging parkland with palm-lined fairways, deep bunkers, and a famous water-guarded 18th hole.

North Course Par 71 • 5,752 m

Shorter and undulating with natural ravines (barrancas) intersecting the holes. Requires precision and shot shaping.

West Course Par 72 • 5,971 m

Carved into pine-covered hills with dramatic elevation changes, blind tee shots, and mountain scenery.

🚗 Logistics & Access

• Direct Luton Flights: Multiple daily flights from LTN to Alicante (ALC) via easyJet and Ryanair (approx 2h 35m).
• Airport Transfer: 105 km (60 mins) down the modern AP-7 / RM-12 motorways.
• Weather Guarantee: Murcia has the warmest and driest March climate in mainland Europe.

🥘 Food & Authenticity

Cliff-Edge Seafood & Murcian Flavours • La Cala Restaurant: Set directly on a cliff edge above a secluded Mediterranean cove, serving freshly caught local fish and paella.
• Amapola: Signature Murcian gastronomy and local Jumilla wines.
Cabo de Palos & Los Belones (5–10 mins) • Cabo de Palos Harbour: Traditional fishing village famous for Caldero Murciano (rich slow-cooked rockfish and rice stew served with homemade alioli).
• Los Belones: Bustling Spanish village with authentic tapas bars, local charcuterie, and friendly tavern atmosphere.

5. Praia D'El Rey & West Cliffs

Silver Coast / Óbidos, Portugal
£480 – £590 per person (3N + 3 rounds)
✈️ Lisbon (LIS) (55 mins drive)
☀️ March Weather: 17.8°C high / 10.1°C low (7.0 hrs sun)
⛳ Golf: 2 World Top-100 Coastal Courses (36 Holes)
🏨 Hotel: Marriott 5* Oceanfront & Golf Village

⛳ 2 World-Class Coastal Courses

West Cliffs Golf Links Par 72 • 6,382 m

Designed by Cynthia Dye. Ranked among the top 15 courses in continental Europe. Rolling coastal dunes, native scrub, and sweeping ocean views on all 18 holes.

Praia D'El Rey Golf Course Par 73 • 6,423 m

Designed by Cabell B. Robinson. A brilliant blend of front-9 pine forest parkland and a breathtaking back-9 playing directly along the Atlantic cliffs.

🚗 Logistics & Access

• Direct Luton Flights: easyJet & Ryanair fly LTN ⇄ Lisbon (LIS) daily (approx 2h 40m).
• Airport Transfer: 85 km (55 mins) north on the easy A8 motorway.
• Atmosphere: Rugged, dramatic coastal scenery and authentic medieval Portugal.

🥘 Food & Authenticity

Fresh Silver Coast Cuisine • Maré Restaurant: Overlooking the Atlantic beach, serving grilled turbot, spider crab, and clams.
• Emprata: Regional Portuguese cuisine paired with local Óbidos and Bairrada wines.
Óbidos & Peniche (15 mins away) • Óbidos (15 mins): Medieval walled fortress village with authentic cobbled taverns, roast suckling pig, and cherry liqueur (Ginjinha) served in chocolate cups.
• Peniche (15 mins): Portugal's premier fishing port; working harbour restaurants with world-class seafood stew (Caldeirada) and grilled sea bass.
