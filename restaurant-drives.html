Nearby food picks

The first three keep the drive short. They lean toward local seafood and well-described cooking rather than chain-style dining.

Salema · Algarve cooking

O Lourenço

Traditional Algarve and Portuguese cooking, with regional petiscos, fish, seafood and cataplana for sharing. My first pick for a proper local meal close by.

Lagos · MICHELIN Guide selected

Avenida

A more polished evening out: tasting menus and à la carte cooking built around maritime flavours and quality meats, overlooking the Lagos marina. The MICHELIN Guide lists it as €€€; it is selected, not starred.
