TrackVision Factory Activation privacy policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Who we are and what this policy covers

TrackVisionAI Ltd, London, United Kingdom, provides TrackVision Factory Activation for factory product commissioning and decommissioning on Android tablets. This policy covers the app and the app data handled by our connected services.

The app is intended for factory workers aged 18 and over. It does not require a personal user account or identify the individual operator. It identifies the configured factory and tablet so that factory operations and support can work.

Contact us about privacy or deletion at support@trackvision.app.

Information the app handles

  • Device registration: Android ID, configured device serial and label, app version, and production line, used to identify and administer the tablet.
  • Factory operations: scanned product identifiers, purchase-order references, commissioning and decommissioning events, timestamps, configured factory and read-point identifiers, and network gateway information, used for product traceability and synchronization.
  • Support: help requests and messages, ticket references, active order and product context, device details, and queue status, used to investigate and resolve problems.
  • Diagnostics and crashes: app and device versions; memory, storage and battery state; scanner and network information, including gateway and Wi-Fi name when available; time-zone settings; configuration; operational event history; and exception information, used to maintain reliability. Requested diagnostic responses may include recent app logs.

Support information is sent when someone asks for help or sends diagnostics. Authorised fleet or support administration can also request diagnostics remotely. After a captured app crash, the app can automatically submit a report when it restarts. Offline records can remain on the tablet until synchronization succeeds.

Camera scanning and Google ML Kit

When camera scanning is selected, the app uses camera access to read barcodes. Camera frames are processed locally using Google ML Kit and are not uploaded by the app's barcode-scanning flow. Decoded barcode values are used in factory operations and may be sent to our connected services.

Google ML Kit separately collects technical telemetry, including device and app information, installation identifiers, performance and error information, feature events, and API and input-size metadata, for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google documents HTTPS transmission for this telemetry. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's privacy policy for its handling of this information.

Who receives information

App data is handled by TrackVision, the customer organisation responsible for the configured factory, and service providers supporting delivery, hosting and support of the service. ML Kit telemetry is sent to Google. We do not sell app data or provide it to advertising recipients.

Device and factory identifiers connect operational and diagnostic records to a tablet and factory. They do not establish the identity of the individual using that tablet.

Retention and deletion

TrackVision-held app data is automatically deleted within 90 days of collection. This applies to app data held by TrackVision; it does not set Google's independent retention periods for ML Kit telemetry or the customer organisation's retention of records it holds separately.

The app also keeps configuration, operational queues, support records and a bounded diagnostic history locally on the tablet. Removing the app or clearing its data does not by itself delete records already sent to connected services.

To request deletion of TrackVision-held app data, email support@trackvision.app and identify the relevant factory and tablet, using its device label or serial where available. No personal app account is needed. Do not send a factory setup QR code, API key or other credential. We may ask for enough information to verify that the request is authorised and locate the relevant records.

You can use the same address for privacy questions or requests to access or correct information. For information held independently by your organisation, contact its administrator; for Google's handling of telemetry, consult the Google information linked above.

Permissions and choices

Camera access is needed only for camera scanning. The app also supports a compatible external barcode scanner. Asking for help and sending a support message are user-initiated actions; device registration, synchronization, automatic crash reporting and remotely requested diagnostics are part of the managed service.
