Longer walk · better food fit

Nazari

Mediterranean cooking with Algarve and Portuguese ingredients, plus Greek, Italian and Middle Eastern influences. This is the walk I’d consider if you want a more interesting meal without driving, but it is not a quick stroll.

28 min · 2.0 km Walking route via N125 and EM537; consider the route and lighting for the return
