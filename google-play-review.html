TrackVision Factory Activation
Google Play reviewer access

Dedicated development factory and sample labels. Open this page on a second screen or print the images so the Android device can scan them. Internet access and camera permission are required.

1. Set up the app

  1. Install TrackVision Factory Activation from the Google Play review build and open it.
  2. Tap Scan Your Factory Code, then select Use camera and scan the setup QR below. Review the details and tap Save. The app restarts.
  3. No account, password, payment, or initial PIN is required. If prompted for a zone, enter REVIEW. Confirm UTC (+00:00) and choose Tablet camera.
Show factory setup QR

Factory: /414/0715353548023. This setup QR can be used on additional review devices.

2. Review activation

  1. Open Activate an item and select either sample order below.
  2. Expand an unused label pair. Show and scan its hang tag first, then show and scan its fabric label. Only one code is shown at a time. Allow the result to sync while online.
  3. Review the active count and item status. Each pair represents one item; ten pairs are supplied for each order.

For repeat checks, use an unused pair. To test reversal, choose Deactivate an item and scan a fabric label activated in this review; stay online for the result. Existing active items can also be inspected without creating another activation.
