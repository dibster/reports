Temperature criteria

The warmer Spanish reference stations average around 17–18°C daily highs. The eastern Algarve is around 16–17°C. The shortlist now requires a regional average daytime high of at least 16°C in both January and February.

A monthly average daily high averages each day’s maximum temperature. It is not an all-day temperature or a forecast. Station location and climate period limit comparisons of small differences. Wind exposure, sunshine and heating also affect comfort.

01 / Dénia: candidate for one stay

Costa Blanca

Dénia · Altea

17.0 / 17.6°CJanuary / February average highsAlicante city · 1981–2010

Town character

Dénia has a permanent food market, a former fishing neighbourhood and documented winter restaurant activity. Altea has a hilltop old town, waterfront, galleries and arts institutions. These features support different town experiences; proximity to Benidorm alone does not determine either town’s atmosphere.

British resort / expat influence

Both towns have international residents and visitors. British pub dominance has not been measured. Local reporting puts UK nationals at about 2.8% of Dénia’s registered residents at the end of 2024. This figure does not measure winter tourists or neighbourhood-level atmosphere.

Winter restaurants and services

Dénia’s council recorded 43 venues in its February–March 2026 tapas route. The 2027 programme and opening dates are unconfirmed. Altea’s Bon Vent publishes September–June hours; Oustau’s seasonal timetable omits January–February.

Walking, dogs and the house

Dénia’s centre and Baix la Mar provide access to town restaurants; Marineta Cassiana is a coastal search area. Outer Les Rotes is farther from the centre. In Altea, walking distances and gradients from hillside villas and Altea Hills require property-level checks.

Temperature and journey: Alicante is south of both towns and especially distant from Dénia. These are regional reference highs, not exact local normals or proof of an advantage over La Herradura. Dénia 17h04 · Altea 17h17.

02 / Bay and promenade

Costa Tropical

La Herradura · Almuñécar

16.8 / 17.7°CJanuary / February average highsMálaga Airport · 1981–2010

Town character

La Herradura is arranged around a bay with a waterfront promenade. Almuñécar is the larger town comparison. La Herradura remains under consideration for a second stay, subject to accommodation and winter service checks.

British resort / expat influence

The area has international residents and visitors. British visitor share has not been verified. The assessment is based on the bay and town layout; there is insufficient evidence to rank its social atmosphere above Dénia’s.

Winter restaurants and services

Winter restaurant opening and choice within walking distance of a rental remain unverified. No house has been confirmed to meet all requirements.

Walking, dogs and the house

The promenade and public town streets provide routes for accompanied dog walks; beach access is separate. Garden villas above the bay may involve steep walks. Exact routes, gradients and pavements need checking.

Temperature and journey: Málaga is a south-coast benchmark, not a measured La Herradura microclimate. The route is just over a strict 20-hour driving limit. La Herradura 20h08 · slightly over cap.

03 / Historic town; lagoon coast

Eastern Algarve

Tavira · Cabanas · Fuseta · Cacela Velha

16.3 / 17.0°CJanuary / February average highsFaro · 1991–2020

Town character

Tavira has a historic centre and riverfront. Fuseta is a smaller lagoon-front town. A garden-house listing has been identified in Cabanas. Evidence for Cacela Velha as a long-stay base is limited; it remains a day-visit option.

British resort / expat influence

British visitor shares have not been verified for these towns. Neighbourhood-level atmosphere and winter restaurant choice require further assessment.

Winter restaurants and services

Tavira is the first location to investigate for winter services and dining. Evidence of winter restaurant choice in the smaller villages is more limited. Summer photographs do not establish winter opening.

Walking, dogs and the house

Tavira is on a river; Cabanas and Fuseta face a lagoon. Mainland waterfront walks are different from walking directly onto an ocean beach. Boats, island beaches, saltpans and dog access need separate checks.

Temperature and journey: Faro is a regional climate reference. The measured Tavira journey exceeds the 20-hour driving limit before stops. It is retained as a comparison. Tavira 21h00 · over cap.

04 / Low regional rainfall

Cabo de Gata

Agua Amarga · San José

16.9 / 17.6°CJanuary / February average highsAlmería Airport · 1981–2010

Town character

Agua Amarga and San José are separate coastal villages in the Cabo de Gata area. Their rocky coastline differs from Cap Ferret’s pine-backed Atlantic setting. Winter restaurant choice and social atmosphere remain insufficiently verified.

British resort / expat influence

No reliable visitor-nationality figures or winter atmosphere assessment were established for either village.

Winter restaurants and services

La Villa is in Agua Amarga; 4nudos is in San José. Published seasonal information does not confirm January–February operation or the range of restaurants available for a long stay.

Walking, dogs and the house

No house-and-garden match or complete daily dog-walking route has been verified in either village. Access rules on the protected coast need checking before selecting a rental.

Temperature and journey: Almería averages 24 / 25mm of January / February rain, below most earlier reference stations. These rainfall figures do not establish wind shelter or higher temperatures at a particular cove. Agua Amarga 20h04 · just over cap.

05 / Atlantic beaches; access restrictions

Costa de la Luz

Conil · Roche · Zahara de los Atunes

16.0 / 16.8°CJanuary / February average highsCádiz · 1981–2010

Town character

Conil town is the first location to assess for walking access to restaurants. Roche has pinewoods and garden-villa developments. A town-edge house listing has been identified in Zahara. Restaurant and beach access must be checked separately for each location.

British resort / expat influence

Visitor nationality shares have not been verified. Descriptions of local character are assessments. Restaurant walking distances from private villa developments remain a separate requirement.

Winter restaurants and services

Pradillo in Zahara has a published schedule covering 2026; 2027 opening dates are unconfirmed. Winter restaurant opening in Conil and walking access from Roche rentals still need verification.

Walking, dogs and the house

Conil’s published rules allow animals on urban beaches outside May–October, while natural beaches are prohibited without authorisation. Its animal ordinance requires dogs over 20kg to be muzzled with a resistant non-extending lead. Rules cannot be carried across to Zahara.

Temperature and journey: Cádiz is a regional climate reference. Wind exposure needs checking at each property, along with its exact address, garden boundaries, heating and walking routes to open restaurants. Conil 20h55 · over cap.

Accommodation / outstanding checks

Rental requirements.

Required features

  • Private house with an exclusive garden, with boundaries checked.
  • Roughly 15 minutes’ real walk to open restaurants and coast, including gradient and pavements.
  • Written pet acceptance, effective heating, and a sunny sheltered outdoor space.
  • Confirmed winter availability, total long-stay price, utilities and cancellation terms.

Unverified details

No property has been verified against all requirements. Listing descriptions need confirmation of pet terms, garden boundaries and walking routes. River or lagoon access is not equivalent to access to an ocean beach.
