What remains / before selecting a house

A short, property-specific checklist.

Confirm the actual home

  • Exclusive garden and current photographs of gates, fence heights and gaps.
  • Written acceptance of both pets, including indoor rules and the complete fee.
  • Effective heating and hot water, with equipment in each bedroom and living space.
  • Exact entrance and measured walking routes to the coast and winter-open restaurants.

Confirm the complete contract

  • Dated total, cleaning, utilities or caps, taxes and refundable deposit.
  • Eligibility for 28 nights: a “monthly” offer may require 31 nights or much longer.
  • Cancellation deadline, refund amount and balance-payment timing.
  • Host approval and final availability before treating any price screen as secured.

Evidence distinguishes live review-screen quotes, published tariffs, listing claims, measured routes and historical guest reports. Listings and linked route results can change.
